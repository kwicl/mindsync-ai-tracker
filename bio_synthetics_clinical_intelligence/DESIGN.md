## Brand & Style

The design system projects clinical authority balanced with progressive artificial intelligence capabilities. Tailored for enterprise health systems, clinicians, researchers, and biotechnology operators, the aesthetic synthesizes deep organic greens with sharp computational clarity. It avoids sterile, clinical coldness by introducing vitalizing verdant hues, structured dark emerald foundations, and high-precision data visualizations.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Tech Minimalism** with **Tonal Glass Layers**. It leverages deeply saturated bio-slate backgrounds, refined translucent overlays, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy. The emotional response is one of surgical precision, computational speed, trustworthy institutional reliability, and luminous optimism in preventative healthcare insights.

## Colors

The core palette is anchored by the deep organic obsidian neutral `#0C110D`, establishing an immersive, distraction-free environment that maximizes readability for analytical dashboards and narrative landing pages. 

- **Primary (`#32884D`)**: An energetic, biological green representing health intelligence, system readiness, and positive diagnostic telemetry. Used for high-impact calls-to-action, active indicators, and critical positive confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#AFCDB8`)**: A pale crystalline sage used for subtle glowing highlights, secondary borders, telemetry badge backgrounds, and supportive typography.
- **Tertiary / Vital Pulse (`#EA4C89`)**: An acute diagnostic magenta-rose, reserved specifically for critical anomalies, physiological pulse metrics, urgency triggers, and dynamic biological inflection points.
- **Neutral (`#0C110D`)**: A dark bio-forest slate underpinning all surface layers, providing substantially deeper contrast than generic cold grays.

### Application Guidelines
- Backgrounds transition smoothly from pure base canvas `#0C110D` into elevated card surfaces `#141D16`.
- High-contrast text `#FFFFFF` sits strictly on container surfaces with at least 8:1 contrast ratios.
- Gradient fills are limited to directional linear glows: blending `rgba(50, 136, 77, 0.25)` to `transparent` on prominent metric displays and active analytical panels.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Space Grotesk** for display headers, data readouts, and operational badges, providing crisp geometry, technical legibility, and architectural precision. Body copy and clinical notations are anchored by **Inter**, chosen for its extreme clarity at small scales, neutral character, and exceptional tabular legibility across dense medical data points.

### Hierarchy & Composition Rules
- Large numerical indicators, percentages, and biometric outputs leverage `metric-display` with tabular numbers enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Section markers, status chips, and overlines must strictly use uppercase with `label-sm` or `label-md`, accompanied by subtle tracking.
- Hero and major display titles should enforce tight line-height to maintain cohesive typographic blocks against data-dense side-panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout operates on a standard 12-column grid for desktop views (max container width: 1280px) and a consolidated 4-column layout for mobile surfaces. The pacing balances high-density health analytics dashboards with expansive, breathable marketing and onboarding flows.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapting Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns, `gutter` set to 1.5rem (24px), outer canvas margins at 3rem (48px). Cards span in units of 3, 4, 6, or 12.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins. Analytics matrices collapse from multi-column grids to paired split cards.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` of 1rem (16px), and screen margins of 1.25rem (20px). All metrics stack sequentially into full-width horizontal tracking cards.

Horizontal padding inside panels adheres strictly to the rhythmic steps: `space-sm` (8px) for badge internals, `space-md` (16px) for basic forms, and `space-xl` (40px) for prominent hero dashboard containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural, luminous layering rather than heavy drop shadows, reflecting a modern software console aesthetic:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base`: Pure canvas background `#0C110D`.
  - `Surface 1 (Cards & Data Cells)`: `#141D16` overlaid with a 1px solid stroke of `#213225`.
  - `Surface 2 (Floating Modals & Dropdowns)`: `#18241B` with subtle back-filter blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Outlines & Ghost Borders**: Structural elements avoid hard black borders; instead, they feature 1px hairline borders tinted with `#213225` or translucent mint `rgba(175, 205, 184, 0.12)`.
- **Bio-Luminescence**: Primary active nodes, floating metrics, and interactive triggers project an ambient verdant halo: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(50, 136, 77, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined curvature profile (`roundedness: 2`) that brings soft ergonomics to rigorous computational layouts:

- Standard input fields, actionable secondary buttons, and nested internal cells use base curvature `0.5rem` (8px).
- Analytical cards, KPI modules, and health alert dialogs employ `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px) to create distinctive content framing.
- Master canvas sections and high-level feature containers utilize `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px).
- Operational badges, active status indicators, and pill-styled navigational filters use full pill radiuses (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish diagnostic tags from functional structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#32884D` fill with text in `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` medium. Height: 44px, padding: 0 20px, roundedness: 8px (`rounded-md`). Subtle ambient green hover glow with a slight brightness boost (`#3B9E5A`).
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#141D16` with a crisp 1px stroke of `#213225`. Text in `#AFCDB8`. On hover, the border transitions to `rgba(175, 205, 184, 0.4)` and the background shifts to `#1A281E`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive Action**: Muted deep tint with `#EA4C89` text and hairline border `rgba(234, 76, 137, 0.3)`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) containers with compact height (24px), font `label-sm`.
- **System Normal / Healthy**: Background `rgba(50, 136, 77, 0.12)`, text `#AFCDB8`, featuring a leading 6px pulsing dot in `#32884D`.
- **Critical Telemetry / Pulse**: Background `rgba(234, 76, 137, 0.12)`, text `#EA4C89`, accompanied by high-alert diagnostic icons.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Background `#141D16` bordered by 1px `#213225`. Corner radius `1rem` (16px).
- Internal layout includes a top utility row for metric metadata and status badges, a middle hero row for massive tabular telemetry readouts (`metric-display`), and a bottom slot for comparative micro-graphs or trend deltas.
- Optional top-edge linear highlight using a 1px border gradient transitioning from `transparent` to `rgba(175, 205, 184, 0.25)` to `transparent`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Height 44px, background `#0C110D`, border 1px solid `#213225`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#8EA696`. Focus state exhibits a crisp 1px border ring in `#32884D` with zero outline offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px dimensions, 4px border radius for checkboxes and 50% for radios. Unchecked state: 1px border `#213225`, background `#141D16`. Checked state: background `#32884D` with crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark/indicator.

### Data Lists & Key-Value Telemetry
- Flat stacked list rows divided by 1px subtle horizontal divider rules (`#1B271E`). 
- Left-aligned labels in muted tone `#8EA696` (`body-sm`), right-aligned values in bright `#FFFFFF` (`Space Grotesk`, medium weight). Rows illuminate on hover with a faint background highlight of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
